# 📊 Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard** is an end-to-end Data Analytics project developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing and loss-making products.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- West Region generated the highest sales.
- Technology category contributed the highest revenue.
- Some products generated high sales but low profit due to heavy discounts.
- Corporate customers contributed significantly to total revenue.
- Monthly sales showed seasonal variations.
- Average shipping time remained consistent across most regions.
